## 5. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Playbook

### 5.1 Page-level writing pattern
1. **Answer first**. Every H2/H3 is a real question; the first sentence directly answers it in 40–60 words, then detail follows.
2. **One fact per sentence**, with the entity named ("The Haridwar Ardh Kumbh Mela 2027 begins on 14 January 2027"), never "it" or "this event" when a snippet might be lifted.
3. **Real HTML tables and lists** for dates, routes and comparisons (not images, not scripted-only widgets).
4. **Visible freshness**: "Last verified: [date]" and a changelog at the foot of date and traffic pages.
5. **Attribution**: link the official source for every date and rule. AI systems prefer pages that cite their sources.
6. **Consistency**: the same date, spelling and naming everywhere (site, schema, Google Business Profile, social).

### 5.2 Model answer blocks (copy, then verify)
- **When is the Haridwar Kumbh Mela 2027?**
  The Haridwar Ardh Kumbh Mela 2027 runs from January to April 2027. It opens on Makar Sankranti, 14 January 2027, and concludes on Chaitra Purnima, 20 April 2027, with ten major bathing festivals in between.
- **What are the Amrit Snan dates in Haridwar 2027?**
  The three Amrit Snan dates are 6 March 2027 (Mahashivratri), 8 March 2027 (Phalguna Amavasya) and 14 April 2027 (Mesh Sankranti), as announced by the Mela Administration. These are the days of the Akharas' royal processions and baths.
- **Is the 2027 Haridwar Kumbh an Ardh Kumbh or a Purna Kumbh?**
  It is an Ardh Kumbh, held halfway between two Purna Kumbh Melas. The last Purna Kumbh was in 2021. The government has said the 2027 Ardh Kumbh will be celebrated on the lines of a Purna Kumbh, with Akhara Amrit Snans.
- **Which snan day is best for elderly parents?**
  Quieter days such as 11 February (Basant Panchami) and 7 April (Nav Samvatsar) are generally calmer than 6 February, 6 March, 8 March and 14 April. Crowd levels are planning estimates, so check live updates before travel.
- **Is Nashik's Kumbh also in 2027?** *(optional single line, see §6)*
  Yes, but it is a separate event at a different place and time. This guide covers Haridwar only.

### 5.3 Entity and trust signals
- **Entity home**: an About page for Rudraksh Safar (address, GST/registration, phone, WhatsApp, team bios), `Organization` schema with `sameAs` to Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, and Wikidata if eligible.
- **Named experts**: author and reviewer bios (Haridwar-based guide; a pandit/scholar for tradition content). "Reviewed by" with date.
- **First-hand evidence**: your own photos, route videos, ghat walk-throughs, and a Haridwar ground-team note. This is the strongest defence against AI-generated competitor pages.
- **Third-party corroboration**: reviews on Google Business Profile, mentions on travel and local news sites, YouTube channel with Hindi explainers, helpful (non-spam) answers on Reddit/Quora.

### 5.4 Crawler and AI-access checklist
- `robots.txt` allows Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended.
- Pages are server-rendered or pre-rendered; the answer text is in the initial HTML.
- Submit to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; enable IndexNow.
- XML sitemap with accurate `lastmod`; clean canonicals; `hreflang` for EN/HI.
- Optional: `public/llms.txt` file.
