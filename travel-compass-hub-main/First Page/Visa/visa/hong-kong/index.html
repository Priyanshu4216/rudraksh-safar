Mistakes to avoid for Hong Kong

  • Use the same passport for PAR and travel.
  • Save the PAR slip on your phone.
  • Name spelt differently across the passport, forms and bookings
  • Blurry or cropped scans of the passport page
  • Hotel and flight dates that do not match the visa dates
