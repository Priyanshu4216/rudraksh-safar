Visa questions, answered

Do I need to fill any online visa form myself?

No. Send documents on WhatsApp or email. We fill the forms, file the application and keep you updated. Where a country needs an interview or biometrics, you attend in person.

How fast is the Vietnam visa?

Under 60 minutes on working days during office hours, with complete documents.

How long does a UAE visa take?

About 4 hours on working days during office hours, with complete documents.

Is Thailand visa-free for Indians?

As of 24 Nov 2026, Indian tourists can enter visa-free for up to 30 days, and must file the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card online before arrival. Stays over 30 days need an e-visa. Rules changed several times in 2026, so we re-check before you travel.

Do Indians need a visa for Malaysia in 2027?

Reports on 19 Nov 2026 said Malaysia’s visa-free entry for Indians ends on 31 Dec 2026, unless extended. Until then you need the free MDAC. For 2027 travel, confirm the visa rule with us first.

Do Indians need a visa for Bali?

Indonesia offers an e-VOA or visa on arrival for 30 days. You also complete the All Indonesia Arrival Card and pay the Bali tourist levy.

What documents are needed for a Schengen visa from India?

Typically a valid passport, application form, photos, travel medical insurance, flight and hotel bookings, bank statements, ITR or employment proof and a cover letter. Biometrics are taken at the visa centre.

Is an interview needed for a US visitor visa?

Most applicants must attend an interview in person. We prepare your DS-160, documents and slot.

Can you arrange dummy hotel and flight bookings?

Yes. We arrange genuine, verifiable itinerary reservations (often called dummy tickets) as proof for visa filing only. They must match your real plans.
