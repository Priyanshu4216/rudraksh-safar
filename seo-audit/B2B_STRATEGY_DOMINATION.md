## 3. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Strategy

Modern search behavior is dominated by conversational AI engines (**Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, ChatGPT Search, Claude**). Answer engines synthesize answers from content that satisfies three strict criteria:
1. **Direct Answer Snapshots:** The first 2-3 sentences immediately solve the query with zero filler.
2. **Authoritative Structured Evidence:** Bulleted proof points, comparison tables, and exact numerical parameters (rates, times, capacities).
3. **Verified Entity Schema:** Strong JSON-LD metadata validating corporate registration, GSTIN, physical address, and telephone channels.
